# GLD/GDX 2025–26 Boom-Bust: Anatomy, Volume Profile, and Flow Estimates

**Purpose:** reference template for estimating potential semiconductor (SMH)
correction magnitude. Data: `daily_close` via Flight service (OHLCV through
2026-11-16), plus public flow/AUM anchors (SEC 10-Qs, World Gold Council,
Morningstar/Bloomberg/ETF.com flow reports) cited inline.

---

## 1. Executive summary

- The "March top" was actually a **two-part top**: blow-off climax **2026-01-29/30**
  (GLD intraday 509.70, close 495.90; GDX intraday 113.50) → one-month distribution
  rally to a marginal secondary high (**GDX peak close 115.84 on 2026-02-27**,
  GLD secondary close-high 483.75–490.02 Feb 27–Mar 2) → **the March breakdown**
  (Iran-war shock, real yields +37bp, gold worst month since Feb 1983).
- **GLD:** +39% Oct 1→peak; peak→now **-28.8%** (352.93, still making new lows
  9.5 months later). **GDX:** +51% Oct 1→peak; peak→trough **-38.9%** (Jul 20,
  ~5 months); now -31.8%.
- **Flows at the pivot:** GLD took in ~$11–12B of share creations during the
  markup (353.7M→~381M shares), then bled a **record -$8.5B in March alone**
  (shares 380M→366.4M) and **-$14.4B cumulative Mar 1–Jul 16**. GDX: ~+$2.3B
  net in Oct–Feb, then -$0.66B in March ($1.3B gross redemptions) and another
  ~-$0.56B Apr–Jun.
- **SMH mapping:** SMH peaked 2026-06-22 @668.91, is already **-33.1%** at the
  Nov 5 low (447.73) — tracking between GLD (-29%) and GDX (-39%) analogs.
  A GDX-magnitude correction implies **~408**, which coincides with SMH's
  largest run-up volume shelf (**POC ~395, HVN 388–414**). Timing analog:
  GDX peak→final-low was ~4.7 months; SMH peak+4.7mo ≈ **mid-November — now**.

### Anatomy read-outs

- **Climax signature:** 3–4× trend-stage volume, intraweek spike high, closes
  near the low — GDX's *largest-ever week by volume* (270.9M, Jan 26–30) closed
  -12%. Volume climax preceded the nominal price top by ~4 weeks.
- **The fatal stage is not the climax — it's the post-rally breakdown.** Both
  ETFs made their nominal top 3–4 weeks after the volume climax, on declining
  volume, then broke on an exogenous shock.
- **Two bear rallies** (+7–17% then +9–45%) trapped dip-buyers before the real
  low. GDX's final low came ~**4.7 months** after peak; GLD (the heavier,
  more crowded asset) is *still* falling at month 9.5.
- **Full round trip:** GDX now trades ~79 — its Oct–Mar volume profile POC
  (77.1). The parabola fully retraced to its volume base.

### GLD vs GDX flow asymmetry

GDX's drawdown (-39%) was deeper than GLD's (to date) despite *smaller* flow
reversal — miner-equity leverage (operational leverage on lower gold +
higher energy costs) dominates fund-flow mechanics. For semis, the equity-beta
analog (GDX) is the more relevant template than the metal analog (GLD).

### SMH anatomy so far vs the gold template

### Magnitude estimate

- **GDX template (-39% peak→trough):** SMH ~408. **GLD template (-29%, still
  open):** SMH ~475 — already breached at the Nov 5 low (447.7).
- Confluence: SMH run-up **HVN shelf 388–414 / POC ~395** ≈ -38 to -42% off
  peak → **central estimate for the correction floor: ~400–420 (-37% to -40%)**,
  with tail risk to the 336–350 shelf (-48%) if the round-trip completes like GDX.
- **Timing:** GDX peak→final-low ≈ 4.7 months → SMH analog lands **~mid-November
  (now)**; GLD's slower bleed implies the *final* low could lag into Q1 2027.
- **Flow signal:** the key gold-analog tell was redemptions *beginning* at the
  break and accelerating for months. SMH's first real redemption wave started
  mid-Oct — i.e., the capitulation phase has likely just begun, consistent with
  a low forming in the ~400–420 zone over the next 1–2 months rather than an
  already-completed bottom at 447.7.
- **Bear-rally expectation:** per template, expect a +15–35% reflex rally off
  whatever becomes the capitulation low (GDX did it twice: +17%, +34%), then a
  months-long secondary decline that retests/nears it.

## 6. Caveats

- GLD "estimated" AUM cells interpolate NAV/share from `daily_close` (GLD trades
  at a small premium/discount to NAV; error <1%).
- GDX implied share counts from vendor AUM snapshots are inconsistent with
  reported net flows (different snapshot conventions); GDX figures are stated
  in flow dollars, not shares.
- Volume profile bins daily volume uniformly across the H-L range — a standard
  approximation, not true tick-level volume-at-price.
- The semi mapping is an analogy, not a forecast; the macro driver differs
  (gold broke on a war/oil/real-yield shock; semis on AI-capex digestion).
