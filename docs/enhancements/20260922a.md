**Yahoo Finance tickers used:**

| Series | Yahoo Code | Notes |
|--------|------------|-------|
| 10-Year Treasury Yield | `^TNX` | CBOE 10-Year Treasury Yield Index |
| WTI Crude Oil | `CL=F` | Front-month WTI futures (standard spot proxy) |
| S&P 500 | `SPY` | SPDR S&P 500 ETF |
| Semiconductors | `SMH` | VanEck Semiconductor ETF |
| Short-term rate (discount-rate proxy) | `^IRX` | 13-week T-bill yield. **No clean Yahoo ticker exists for the actual Fed Discount Window rate.** `^IRX` (and Fed Funds) track policy rates closely; the true Discount Rate is set by the Fed and rarely changes independently. |

Data period: Jan 2020 – Sep 2026 (daily closes).

### Key Correlation Findings

**Price/Yield Levels (full period)**  
- `SMH` ↔ `SPY`: **0.94** (very high, as expected)  
- `TNX_10Y` ↔ `SPY`: **0.73**  
- `TNX_10Y` ↔ `SMH`: **0.63**  
- `TNX_10Y` ↔ `WTI`: **0.52**  
- `IRX_3M` ↔ `TNX_10Y`: **0.92** (tight curve relationship)  
- Oil vs equities: only modest positive (~0.26–0.31)

**Daily Returns/Changes** (more relevant for short-term co-movement)  
- `SMH` ↔ `SPY`: **0.81**  
- Everything else is weak (0.07–0.23). Oil and yields show only mild positive correlation on daily moves.

### Design Notes / Interpretation Tips
- **Levels** correlations are high for equities/yields because both rose together from the 2020 lows through the post-COVID recovery and 2022–2023 rate-hike cycle.  
- **Returns** correlations are much lower — daily moves are noisy.  
- Recent (2026) commentary has noted unusually high short-term oil–10Y yield correlation (sometimes >0.9 on 1-month windows) during geopolitical spikes; the 60-day rolling chart captures those episodes.  
- Semiconductors (SMH) are more sensitive to rates/discounting than the broad market because of long-duration growth characteristics, yet still move very closely with SPY.  
- For a pure “discount rate” series you would normally pull the Fed Discount Rate or Effective Fed Funds from FRED (`DISCOUNT` or `FEDFUNDS`); Yahoo does not carry a clean equivalent.
